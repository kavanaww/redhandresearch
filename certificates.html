What every lot is tested for

Identity and purity

HPLC and mass spectrometry confirm that the compound is what the label says it is, and report its purity. The figure for your lot is on its CoA.

Sterility

Independent sterility testing screens each lot for microbial contamination.

Bacterial endotoxins

LAL (Limulus amebocyte lysate) testing per lot detects endotoxins that can interfere with in-vitro work.

Lot consistency

Multi-point verification checks consistency across vials within a lot and across sequential lots of the same compound.

Common questions

What does "ISO/IEC 17025 accredited" mean?

ISO/IEC 17025:2017 is the international standard for testing and calibration laboratories. Accreditation means the lab's methods, equipment and staff have been independently audited against that standard. It's the highest level of third-party lab credentialing available.

Is a CoA included with my order?

CoAs are sent on request rather than packed with every shipment. Email us your order number and product name and we'll send it, usually the same day.

Can I see a CoA before I buy?

Yes. Email the product name and we'll share the most recent lot's CoA.

Where is the lot number?

Printed on the vial label. Your order confirmation email also lists what shipped.
