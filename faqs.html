Testing & certificates

How are products tested?

Every lot is tested by an independent, U.S.-based ISO/IEC 17025:2017-accredited laboratory for identity and purity (HPLC and mass spectrometry), with sterility and endotoxin screening per lot. We don't do in-house testing; all analytical data comes from accredited outside labs.

How do I get a Certificate of Analysis?

Email with the product name and your order number or lot number (printed on the vial). We send the lot-specific CoA as a PDF, usually within one business day. You can also request the current lot's CoA before you order. See Testing & CoAs.

Research use only

What does "research use only" mean?

Everything we sell is designated strictly for laboratory-based, in-vitro scientific research by qualified people. Our products are not for human or veterinary administration, and no diagnostic, therapeutic or clinical use is implied or permitted. Purchasing means you agree to use them only for lawful research.

Who can buy?

Researchers, scientists and institutions engaged in lawful scientific inquiry who are at least 21 years old. By ordering you confirm both.

Are these products legal to purchase?

The compounds we sell are legal to purchase for research purposes in the United States. Rules vary by state, and it's your responsibility to confirm compliance with local, state and federal law before ordering.

Storage & handling

How should compounds be stored?

Lyophilized peptides: −20 °C, sealed, dry and away from light. Once reconstituted: refrigerate at 2–8 °C and use within the window noted on the CoA. Storage details are on each product page and vial label.

What is the shelf life?

It varies by compound. Properly stored lyophilized peptides are generally stable for about 24 months. Dates are printed on each label and supported by the lot's stability data.

What is bacteriostatic water for?

It's sterile water with 0.9% benzyl alcohol, commonly used in laboratories to reconstitute lyophilized peptides. Some compounds need a different solvent; check the compound-specific documentation.
