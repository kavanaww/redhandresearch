Return policy

Overview

Because of the nature of research compounds and the analytical standards needed to maintain lot integrity, returns are limited. Review product documentation before ordering, and contact us first if you have questions about a product's suitability for your research.

Opened or used products

We cannot accept returns on any product that has been opened, reconstituted or otherwise used. Once packaging integrity is compromised, lot traceability and analytical validity can't be guaranteed.

Unopened products

Unopened products in original, undamaged packaging may be returned within 14 days of delivery if: the lot label is intact and legible; you request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) from within those 14 days; and the return ships with that RMA. Returns without an RMA are not accepted. Return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the return is due to our error, and a restocking fee of up to 15% may apply. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days of our receipt and inspection.

Incorrect or defective products

If you receive the wrong product, or a product that doesn't match its lot-specific Certificate of Analysis, contact us within 7 days of delivery. If confirmed, we'll send a replacement or issue a full refund at no cost to you, including return shipping where applicable. Keep all original packaging and documentation.

Cancellations

Refund timing

Approved refunds are processed within 3–5 business days of confirmation. Your bank may take an additional 5–10 business days to show it.
